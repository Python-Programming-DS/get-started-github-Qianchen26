# What are you interested in learning? 
In general, I'm interested in improving urban resilience through data-driven (AI) approach.
# What are you working on? 
I'm designing a python based discrete event simulator. The first version, which is called PyRebuild, is designated for assigning construction crews to damaged houses after earthquakes. This simulator is beneficial because it considers $three$ real-world constraints:
- 1. The complete damage reports (e.g., total amount of damaged houses, damage levels) may not be immediately available when earthquake happens. Instead, damage reports may be unmasked gradually (in batches).
  2. The simulator compares heuristics strategies (e.g., Longest-Job-First, Shortest-Job-First) and AI approach (e.g., Soft-Actor-Critic), so that decison makers can choose the optimal allocation policy.
  3. The simulator emphaszies "UNCERTAINTY": it simulates the dropout and delay of construction crews.
